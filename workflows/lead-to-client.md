# Workflow: Lead to Client

1. **Human/Coordinator intake:** define service, geography, ICP, target, objective.
2. **Lead Hunter:** research organisations and observable needs; record source URLs, dates, evidence, confidence, unknowns.
3. **Lead Qualifier:** score fit, service match, need, urgency, commercial signals, reachability, evidence quality; classify PRIORITY / NURTURE / REJECT / NEEDS MORE EVIDENCE.
4. **Sales Strategist:** independently challenge evidence and recommendation.
5. **Follow-up Manager:** prepare personalised draft; do not send.
6. **Meeting Coordinator:** check availability if requested; do not book.
7. **Proposal Agent:** draft after discovery using only human-provided commercial terms.
8. **Human:** approve/edit/reject external communication, booking, proposal, and commitments.

Each lead should have stable ID, company, sources, evidence date, score, confidence, owner, status, next action, and approval state. Stop when identity or evidence is ambiguous. Qualification is not a commitment.
