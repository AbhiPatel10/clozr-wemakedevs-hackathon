# Workflow: Sales to Business Analysis

Trigger: human decides a qualified opportunity should enter discovery/BA.

Required intake: opportunity ID; client context if authorised; business problem/outcomes; discovery notes; user roles; desired features/constraints; timeline/budget only if explicitly provided; unknowns/risks.

1. Coordinator packages validated context.
2. Human confirms sharing with BA.
3. BA Manager creates project ID and intake.
4. Requirements Analyst separates facts, assumptions, unknowns, exclusions.
5. Architect and Estimator prepare their analyses.
6. QA Validator independently reviews.
7. BA Manager consolidates revisions and prepares internal handoff; client-facing SOW is separate.
8. Human BA Head reviews and approves.

Sales qualification is not requirements sign-off and does not authorise scope, architecture, pricing, delivery dates, or development.
